Made of pixels & a little magic.

The interactive capsule machine is made from original procedural Three.js geometry, with a moving handle, opening door, and individual tumbling capsules.

The 36 original fantasy creatures, capsule illustration, and fallback machine image were created for Pocket Pals with OpenAI ImageGen. Shiny pals use alternate palettes and sparkle effects.

Pixelify Sans is by the Pixelify Sans Project Authors, under the SIL Open Font License. Icons are by Lucide. Optional sounds are synthesized in your browser.

Your collection stays in this browser. It does not sync between devices or different website addresses. Clearing site data removes your saved pals.
